# hello, internet

<p class="post-meta mono">by M. Thio Saputra · 2026-09-08 · Banjarmasin, WITA</p>

Halo! This is the first post from a new home on the web:
**[thio.cc.cd](https://thio.cc.cd)** — the personal site of
**M. Thio Saputra**, known online as **Flessan**.

If you've followed my work before, you've probably met me through
[@flessan on GitHub](https://github.com/flessan), through
[GDIPS](/gdips) — the Geometry Dash Indonesia Private Server I founded — or
through one of the many rooms of what I call
[the internet of Thio](./internet-of-thio.md). This site gathers all of it
under one roof.

## Who is writing here

I'm a self-taught developer and a student in the
*Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak* (Software Engineering) program at
SMKN 4 Banjarmasin, in South Kalimantan, Indonesia. My practice moves between
web development, Linux, automation, open source, Wikipedia, school
organizations, and music.

One thing about the name: my full name is **Muhammad Thio Saputra**. People
shorten it to "M." — and honestly, I don't really like that, so instead of
adding "M." everywhere I've mostly just hidden it. Online, I'm Flessan.
Also Fless, Flethio, or Thio2009_, depending on which corner of the internet
you found first.

## What this site will hold

- **Projects** — the full rack, from [GDIPS](/gdips) and
  [slate-de](./slate-de.md) to small tools like
  [teksreceh](./teksreceh.md), with links to everything that's live.
- **Writing** — build notes and stories, right here in the blog.
- **Now & timeline** — a [/now page](/now) for what I'm currently building,
  learning, and listening to, and a [timeline](/timeline) of how I got here.
- **Doors** — an [ecosystem page](/ecosystem) linking every room of the
  network: the lab, the games, the music, the tools, and the archive.

## Why another site?

Because I learn by making things real. My first website was coded at age 15
using ZArchiver and Chrome on a phone. Since then there's been
[flessan.pages.dev](https://flessan.pages.dev) — the first little site — then
[thio.qzz.io](https://thio.qzz.io), the main house. This domain,
**thio.cc.cd**, is the newest room: a faster, cleaner home built with
VitePress, and a place to write in both Bahasa Indonesia and English.

Old work is part of the work. The previous sites stay online, screenshots and
all — nothing gets buried, everything gets a door.

## Say hello

The fastest way to reach me is [tio@cc.cc](mailto:tio@cc.cc). You can also
find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/flessan),
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/flethio) or
[Threads](https://www.threads.com/flethio), or join the
[GDIPS Discord](https://discord.gg/6HEyQBcM6E) campfire.

Thanks for stopping by. Let's build something amazing together.

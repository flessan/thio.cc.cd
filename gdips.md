<section>
  <SectionHead num="01" label="The idea">
    Not the biggest. Not the fanciest. <em>Built with heart.</em>
  </SectionHead>

  <div class="gdips-cols">
    <div class="gdips-copy">
      <p>
        GDIPS started the way most of my favorite things start: as a list. In
        July 2025 I published <em>Freedom-List</em> — "Pomni Pomni Pomni" — a
        directory of free Geometry Dash servers. That list became a doorway
        into the GDPS scene, and by April 2026 it grew into a server of its
        own: <strong>GDIPS Reborn</strong>, an open-source private server
        built together with its community.
      </p>
      <p>
        The rules are simple and they shape everything: collaborative
        moderation, creativity-focused rules, and an Asia-based CDN so
        players in Indonesia get a smoother ride. It is a campfire — warm
        enough for those who want to gather around, without pretending to be
        perfect.
      </p>

   <blockquote class="f-quote">
      "We're building a digital campfire, not a perfect one, but warm enough
      for those who want to gather around."
      <cite>— M. Thio Saputra · founder of GDIPS</cite>
      </blockquote>
    </div>

   <div class="f-panel">
    <h3>What's inside</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Basic server functionality &amp; experimental tools</li>
      <li>Asia-based CDN for smoother access</li>
      <li>UI overhaul — a work in progress</li>
      <li>Basic security framework &amp; database optimizations</li>
      <li>Collaborative moderation &amp; creativity-focused rules</li>
    </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
